# 7 ADHD prompt patterns — internal scoring rubric for Step 6

When picking the one action in Step 6, ask which pattern fits the stuck-state. **This is an
internal rubric, not a menu to present** — see Step 6 for the "pick ONE, no menu" rule.

| # | Pattern | When it fits | Concrete action shape |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Task Paralysis Shatterer** | Task feels too big / unclear where to start | "First ridiculously-small step: open `<file>` / dial `<number>` / put cursor at line X. <1 min." |
| 2 | **Dopamine Menu** | Task is boring/admin and energy is low | "Pair the task with a 5-min appetizer (cold water, song, walk) before/after." |
| 3 | **Body Doubling Simulator** | Task needs focus but solo work feels impossible | "I act as your body double — you tell me what you're doing, I check in every 10 min." |
| 4 | **Context-Switch Cleanser** | You just finished something different; brain stuck on the old context | "3-min palate cleanser: stand up, water, one physiological sigh, then start." |
| 5 | **Interest-Based Filter** | Boring admin task with no intrinsic pull | "Gamify by linking to a current hyperfixation, or reframe to a lower-stakes channel (email instead of call)." |
| 6 | **Time-Blindness Auditor** | Estimating duration; will overrun | "Map the 3 hidden sub-tasks usually missed → realistic deadline." |
| 7 | **Open-Loop Externalizer** | Brain full of competing tasks blocking this one | "Dump everything in your head into Now / Later / Trash buckets first." |

Plus one meta-pattern always in your back pocket:

- **Defer-with-scaffolding**: "Snooze to <when> AND I pre-stage <artifact> now so future-you has
  zero prep." Pick this when energy or context is obviously wrong; do NOT pick it as a generic
  safe fallback.

## How to pick (internally)

Score candidates internally on:
- **Fit**: which pattern matches the actual stuck-state of this task?
- **Friction floor**: lowest-friction concrete action that still moves the task forward
- **Context signals**: time-of-day, energy hint, deadline pressure, recent activity

Commit to one action and write it as a single imperative sentence with the exact tangible target
(file path, phone number, sentence to read, app to open). Note the ADHD pattern you used in
parentheses for transparency.

## Example

Task: "call the clinic" at 16:48 with a pre-written script in the task comments.

> **Recommended action:** Dial the clinic in the next 90 seconds and read comment #3 verbatim —
> the script is already written. If voicemail, leave the 4-point list.
> *(Pattern #1: Task Paralysis Shatterer — friction floor is "just dial," and comment #3 means
> there's literally nothing to prep. Office closes in ~12 min; defer-with-scaffolding loses the
> day.)*

That's it. No menu.
