Neuro Toolkit

pilot

A toolkit for brains with ADHD, autism, or both — field-tested ways for everyday challenges.

Start from what you are actually dealing with. Click — and you get methods straight away. If you want to understand why, drill deeper: into mechanisms and the research.

Everyday & body

  • Can't get started (boring tasks)

    You know you must — paperwork, a form, a call — but the brain just won't start, because there's no novelty or reward in it.

  • Everything falls apart — time, mess, organisation

    You're always late, time-blind, drowning in clutter, and can't keep daily logistics or routines together.

  • Dysregulated sleep

    Can't fall asleep, going to bed too late, broken sleep — or all of it at once.

  • Energy and burnout

    Your head signs you up for more than the body can carry; even beloved work in hyperfocus can end in burnout.

  • Noise, light, and crowds physically overwhelm me

    Everyday input — sound, light, textures, crowds — hits too hard and tips you into overload. Below: dampen the input, dose it on purpose, and plan your exit before you hit red.

  • I run out of energy long before the day is done

    Your daily energy is a limited budget — spoons — and pushing past it triggers the crash. Below: pace the day, schedule recharge, and protect capacity before it's gone.

  • I shut down or melt down and can't come back

    When the load tips over you freeze, go silent, or erupt — and ordinary coping stops working. Below: tell shutdown from meltdown, and how to come back down from each.

Mind & emotions

  • Emotions out of control — anger, shame, taking things personally

    Emotions throw you around: you explode, feel shame, think in black-and-white, and sometimes can't even tell what you feel.

  • Losing thoughts / interrupting

    You're afraid you'll forget, so you cut others off; an uncaptured thought 'eats' your working memory for a week.

  • Fear and avoidance

    You delay, dodge, rationalise — anything to not feel the discomfort. The more you fight the fear, the bigger it grows.

  • I can't handle rejection — rejection sensitivity (RSD)

    Rejection and criticism hit you disproportionately hard, and you constantly feel like people are mad at you.

  • I feel broken and worthless

    You beat yourself up, feel never-enough, carry impostor syndrome, and can't accept or like yourself.

  • Life feels empty and meaningless

    Nothing feels good, you feel empty and purposeless — like you're just existing, not living. Sometimes with thoughts that you don't want to be alive.

Self & identity

  • Do I even have this? — self-screening and late adult diagnosis

    You recognise yourself in autism/ADHD/AuDHD traits, look for tests and a diagnosis, and process what it means to find out only now.

  • I feel different and keep pretending — who am I really?

    You mask to fit in, burn out from faking normal, and don't know if your neurodivergence is a flaw or a strength.

  • I want to stop fighting myself and like who I am

    Not every day is a crisis — sometimes the work is accepting your brain and using it as a strength. Below: reframing neurodivergence as difference-not-defect, and building real self-acceptance.

People & relationships

  • I can't connect socially — cues, small talk, loneliness

    You miss social cues, overshare or are too blunt, struggle to make and keep friends, and feel alone.

  • I can't read the cues or find the words in the moment

    Eye contact, unwritten rules, knowing what to say — interaction runs on a code no one taught you. Below: concrete, learnable scripts and prep that take the guesswork out.

  • People drain me, and afterwards I'm wrecked

    You want connection, but interaction leaves you flattened — the social hangover, the 'was I too much?' spiral. Below: pace contact, leave before you crash, and recover without guilt.

  • Dating and relationships aren't working out

    You struggle to find and keep a partner and relationships fall apart — boundaries, trust, the friendzone, loneliness.

  • Sex, porn, and intimacy — shame and compulsion

    Struggling with porn, hypersexuality, or the opposite — low libido, shame around sex, virginity, and intimacy.

Control & direction

  • Addictions — gaming, screens, scrolling, substances

    You can't rein in gaming, the phone, screen dopamine or substances, and the brain keeps offering easy hits.

  • Work and career overwhelm me

    You can't keep up with demands, accommodations or a career path, and feel you're wasting your potential and falling behind.

  • The past holds me — trauma, toxic parents, grief

    You dwell on old wounds, carry toxic childhood relationships and unprocessed loss.

  • Looking for therapy that fits the brain

    Tried therapy and it 'didn't work'? The modality matters enormously for a neurodivergent brain — not every kind fits.

Caregiver mode

  • Supporting a neurodivergent loved one

    You're a parent or partner of someone with ADHD/autism and want to understand and support them better — without burning yourself out.
